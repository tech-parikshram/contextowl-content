## AI Search Basics

### What Is AI Search Visibility? A Plain-English Definition
- **Takeaway:** AI search visibility describes what an AI engine says when someone asks about your category: whether it names you, describes you correctly, recommends you and cites a source.
- **Link:** https://contextowl.com/blog/what-is-ai-search-visibility

### What Is GEO? Generative Engine Optimization, Explained
- **Takeaway:** GEO, short for generative engine optimization, means making your content and brand easier for AI answer engines to find, understand and cite.
- **Link:** https://contextowl.com/blog/what-is-geo-generative-engine-optimization

### GEO vs AEO vs SEO vs LLMO: What Changed and What Didn't
- **Takeaway:** SEO, AEO, GEO and LLMO describe overlapping work aimed at different surfaces: ranked links, direct-answer boxes, generative-engine citations, and how an LLM describes your brand more generally.
- **Link:** https://contextowl.com/blog/geo-vs-aeo-vs-seo

### How ChatGPT Search Finds and Chooses Its Sources
- **Takeaway:** ChatGPT answers from two different places: what it learned during training, or a live web search it runs for that specific question. Only the live-search path involves choosing sources.
- **Link:** https://contextowl.com/blog/how-does-chatgpt-choose-sources

### Is SEO Dead? What the Data on AI Search and Clicks Shows
- **Takeaway:** No, SEO isn't dead. Google's own guidance says its generative AI features are "rooted in" its core ranking and quality systems — SEO fundamentals still feed them directly.
- **Link:** https://contextowl.com/blog/is-seo-dead-because-of-ai-search

### Mentions, Citations and Recommendations Are Three Different Things
- **Takeaway:** A mention is your brand named in the answer text. A citation is a source link attributed to you. A recommendation is the answer actively positioning you as the right choice. These are three separate events — one can happen without the others.
- **Link:** https://contextowl.com/blog/brand-mention-vs-citation-vs-recommendation-ai

### The AI Search Glossary: Terms Marketers Keep Mixing Up
- **Takeaway:** GEO, AEO, LLMO and "AI SEO" are overlapping labels for similar work on different surfaces, not four separate disciplines — see [the full comparison](/blog/geo-vs-aeo-vs-seo) if you need to pick one for a report.
- **Link:** https://contextowl.com/blog/ai-search-glossary

### What Is AEO? Answer Engine Optimization, Without the Hype
- **Takeaway:** AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) means structuring content so a feature that extracts one direct answer — a featured snippet, a voice result, an AI Overview, an AI chat answer — can pull yours out cleanly.
- **Link:** https://contextowl.com/blog/what-is-aeo-answer-engine-optimization

### AI Search vs Traditional Search: How an Answer Gets Made
- **Takeaway:** A traditional results page ranks pages and shows ten links. An AI answer retrieves candidate pages, picks a subset, and writes a short answer that names a few brands directly.
- **Link:** https://contextowl.com/blog/ai-search-vs-traditional-search

### How AI Decides Which Pages to Cite
- **Takeaway:** There's no single, published "citation algorithm" any engine runs. What's documented is a pipeline: retrieve candidate pages, ground the answer in them, cite what got used. The exact ranking inside that pipeline isn't public.
- **Link:** https://contextowl.com/blog/how-ai-decides-which-websites-to-cite

### How Claude Searches the Web and Describes Brands
- **Takeaway:** Claude is made by Anthropic, which operates three separate bots: ClaudeBot (training data), Claude-SearchBot (search indexing) and Claude-User (live, user-triggered fetches). Each is controlled independently in robots.txt — blocking one doesn't affect the others.
- **Link:** https://contextowl.com/blog/how-does-claude-choose-sources-brands

### How Claude, Gemini and Copilot Answer Brand Questions
- **Takeaway:** Claude, Gemini and Copilot each publish some detail about sourcing and leave the rest unconfirmed — this post says exactly where that line falls for each.
- **Link:** https://contextowl.com/blog/how-do-claude-gemini-copilot-choose-sources

### How Google AI Overviews and AI Mode Pick What to Cite
- **Takeaway:** AI Overviews and AI Mode are two separate features built on the same underlying Search systems, but one is a short summary and the other is a longer, exploratory answer.
- **Link:** https://contextowl.com/blog/how-google-ai-overviews-choose-sources

### How Google Gemini Chooses Sources and Describes Brands
- **Takeaway:** Gemini (the standalone app), Google AI Overviews and Google AI Mode are three different products. Google documents AI Overviews and AI Mode together, in detail, on Search Central. The Gemini app itself isn't covered by that documentation at all.
- **Link:** https://contextowl.com/blog/how-does-gemini-choose-sources-brands

### How Perplexity Chooses Its Sources
- **Takeaway:** Perplexity retrieves pages live for most answers and always shows a numbered list of sources — it's built to cite, not just to summarize.
- **Link:** https://contextowl.com/blog/how-does-perplexity-choose-sources

### Prompts, Not Keywords: How Buyers Actually Ask AI
- **Takeaway:** A keyword is a fragment ("project management software"). A prompt is a full question with context attached ("what's a good project management tool for a 5-person remote team on a tight budget"). AI engines answer the second kind.
- **Link:** https://contextowl.com/blog/how-people-search-with-ai-prompts

### Training vs Retrieval: Why AI Knows (or Doesn't) Your Brand
- **Takeaway:** An AI engine can answer a question from what it learned during training, or by retrieving current pages live — these are two separate mechanisms with different failure modes.
- **Link:** https://contextowl.com/blog/how-ai-knows-about-my-brand

### What Is Query Fan-Out and Why It Changes Content Planning
- **Takeaway:** Query fan-out is a model issuing several related searches behind one question before it writes an answer, instead of matching only the literal words typed.
- **Link:** https://contextowl.com/blog/what-is-query-fan-out

### Which AI Engines Should You Prioritise? One Source, Many Engines
- **Takeaway:** There's no one engine to optimize for. Clear, well-structured, fact-stated content travels across most of them from a single source — the differences show up in retrieval and phrasing, not in needing five separate content strategies.
- **Link:** https://contextowl.com/blog/which-ai-search-engines-to-optimize-for

## Measurement

### 'Our AI Visibility Score Is 72': Why One Number Misleads
- **Takeaway:** This is our opinion: a bare AI visibility score, with no disclosed method, is close to meaningless — it can mean almost anything depending on what's underneath it.
- **Link:** https://contextowl.com/blog/ai-visibility-score-meaning

### How to Measure AI Visibility: Metrics That Hold Up
- **Takeaway:** Four metrics hold up across repeated runs: visibility rate, citation rate, share of voice, and sentiment paired with accuracy.
- **Link:** https://contextowl.com/blog/how-to-measure-ai-visibility

### Same Prompt, Different Brand Lists: How Many Runs Do You Need?
- **Takeaway:** Asking the same prompt twice on the same engine almost never returns the same list of brands. One large study found under a 1-in-100 chance of an identical list, and about 1-in-1,000 of an identical list in the same order.
- **Link:** https://contextowl.com/blog/same-prompt-different-results-chatgpt-brands

### Share of Voice in AI Answers: How to Calculate It
- **Takeaway:** AI share of voice is your brand's mentions divided by total mentions across every tracked brand, over the same fixed set of prompts and runs.
- **Link:** https://contextowl.com/blog/ai-share-of-voice-how-to-calculate

### Visibility Rate vs Rank: Can You Rank #1 in ChatGPT?
- **Takeaway:** This is our opinion, built on the evidence below: "rank #1 in ChatGPT" is a category error. Google's results page has a stable rank because the same query returns the same ordered list absent an algorithm update. ChatGPT's answers don't work that way.
- **Link:** https://contextowl.com/blog/can-you-rank-number-1-in-chatgpt

### A Beginner's Guide to Tracking AI Referral Traffic in GA4
- **Takeaway:** GA4 added a native "AI Assistant" channel: as of this writing, Google's own documentation lists it as triggered by referrers from ChatGPT, Gemini, Deepseek, Copilot or Grok — Perplexity and Claude currently land in Referral instead.
- **Link:** https://contextowl.com/blog/track-ai-traffic-in-ga4

### A One-Page AI Visibility Report for a CMO or Client
- **Takeaway:** A one-page AI visibility report needs five things: the finding, the evidence behind it, five numbers, one chart and a short fix list — nothing else.
- **Link:** https://contextowl.com/blog/one-page-ai-visibility-report-template

### Bing AI Performance: Free Citation Data and Grounding Queries
- **Takeaway:** Bing Webmaster Tools' AI Performance report, free in public preview since 10 February 2026, shows total citations, average cited pages, page-level citation activity and grounding queries — the first free, first-party citation dataset from a major engine.
- **Link:** https://contextowl.com/blog/bing-ai-performance-report-grounding-queries

### How to Build a Prompt Set That Represents Your Buyers
- **Takeaway:** A prompt set should cover every funnel stage (awareness, consideration, comparison, decision), mix branded and unbranded wording, and name competitors fairly — not just your own brand.
- **Link:** https://contextowl.com/blog/how-to-build-prompt-list-for-ai-visibility

### How to Build an AI Visibility Benchmark Against Competitors
- **Takeaway:** A benchmark is only fair if every brand in it gets the exact same prompt wording, the same engine, and runs logged on the same day.
- **Link:** https://contextowl.com/blog/how-to-benchmark-ai-visibility-competitors

### How to Read an AI Visibility Report Without Being Fooled
- **Takeaway:** A trustworthy report discloses exactly what it ran: which prompts, which engines, how many runs per prompt, and on what date — without that, a score can't be checked by anyone.
- **Link:** https://contextowl.com/blog/how-to-read-ai-visibility-report

### The AI Attribution Problem: Proxies That Work When Clicks Don't
- **Takeaway:** There is no clean, click-based way to attribute a conversion to an AI answer. The journeys are fragmented across devices, sessions and apps, and a meaningful share of AI-referred visits arrive with no referrer at all.
- **Link:** https://contextowl.com/blog/how-to-attribute-ai-search-traffic

### What Is Citation Share in AI Search?
- **Takeaway:** Citation share is the percentage of citations in an answer (or set of answers) that point to one site, out of all citations shown for the same query — Microsoft's own term for this in Bing Webmaster Tools.
- **Link:** https://contextowl.com/blog/citation-share-ai-search

### What Is a 'Good' AI Visibility Rate? There Is No Universal Answer
- **Takeaway:** There's no universal "good" AI visibility rate. A category with three dominant brands behaves nothing like one with thirty close competitors — the same percentage means something different in each.
- **Link:** https://contextowl.com/blog/what-is-a-good-ai-visibility-score


## Mistakes and Myths

### Blocking GPTBot Isn't Blocking ChatGPT: Should You Block AI Crawlers?
- **Takeaway:** OpenAI, Anthropic and Perplexity each run more than one bot: a training crawler, a search crawler, and a user-triggered fetcher. They are controlled by separate lines in robots.txt and do separate jobs.
- **Link:** https://contextowl.com/blog/should-i-block-ai-crawlers-gptbot

### llms.txt: What It Is, What the Evidence Says, Whether to Bother
- **Takeaway:** llms.txt is a proposed markdown file at a site's root, meant to give AI agents a curated, easy-to-parse map of a site's content, instead of making them crawl and parse full HTML pages.
- **Link:** https://contextowl.com/blog/does-llms-txt-help-ai-search

## Playbooks

### The AI Search Audit: A 10-Step Checklist for Your Site
- **Takeaway:** Ten checks cover the full surface that decides whether AI engines can find, read and cite your site: crawler access, rendering, entities, content, schema, prompts, accuracy, competitors, third-party presence and a Google AI Overviews baseline.

### A robots.txt Guide for AI Search and Training Crawlers
- **Takeaway:** Every major AI company runs at least two separate bots: one for search/citations, one for training, and (for OpenAI, Anthropic and Perplexity) a third for live, user-triggered fetches. Blocking one doesn't affect the others.
- **Link:** https://contextowl.com/blog/robots-txt-ai-crawlers-guide

### Branded Search and AI Visibility: Why Your Name Comes First
- **Takeaway:** Branded search is anyone searching your name, a close variant, or a product only you make. It comes first because an AI engine has to resolve who you are before it can describe or recommend you.
- **Link:** https://contextowl.com/blog/branded-search-ai-visibility

### Citation Gap Analysis: What AI Cites for Rivals, Not for You
- **Takeaway:** A citation gap analysis lists every source an AI engine cites when it talks about your competitors, then checks which of those sources ever cite you.
- **Link:** https://contextowl.com/blog/citation-gap-analysis-ai-search

### Digital PR for AI Search: Earning Mentions Models Pick Up
- **Takeaway:** Across 75,000 brands, Ahrefs found branded web mentions correlate with AI visibility more strongly than any other on-page or authority signal it tested — correlation, not proof that mentions cause visibility.
- **Link:** https://contextowl.com/blog/digital-pr-for-ai-search

### Entity SEO for AI: Make Your Brand Easy to Recognise and Describe
- **Takeaway:** An "entity" is a real-world thing — your company — with a stable identity across the web, separate from any one page's wording about it.
- **Link:** https://contextowl.com/blog/entity-seo-for-ai-search

### How to Write Pages AI Can Quote: Answer-First Content
- **Takeaway:** Answer-first means the direct answer appears in the page's first paragraph, before background, history or a sales pitch — not buried after three paragraphs of context.
- **Link:** https://contextowl.com/blog/answer-first-content-for-ai-search

### Is Your Site Invisible to AI Crawlers? Access and Rendering
- **Takeaway:** robots.txt is a request, not a technical barrier — and the reverse is also true: a page can look "allowed" in robots.txt while a CDN's bot-management setting or a firewall blocks the bot before it ever reaches your server.

### Original Data as a Citation Magnet for Small Teams
